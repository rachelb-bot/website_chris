# Applications

Specs for everything outside the website. Use the outlined SVG logos for print and vinyl so nothing depends on installed fonts. Colors for print: use the hex values from the tokens; ask the printer to match `night` #16302F, `rust` #B9481F and `tan` #E7CF97 by eye from a printed proof, since screens and paper differ.

## Truck

- **Doors:** `badge-off80.svg` at 22–24 in across, centered on the door. Under it, the phone number (530) 368-5077 in Alfa Slab One at 5 in cap height, then "Licensed electrician · CA C-10 #1128029" in Archivo Bold at 1.5 in. Vinyl colors: badge in full color; text in `night` on a white truck, `tan` on a dark truck.
- **Tailgate:** `lockup-light.svg` (or `lockup-dark.svg` on a dark truck) 48 in wide, phone number below it at 6 in, then off80electric.com. Leave the bottom 6 in clear of the bumper.
- **Side panels or box:** the route line (I-80 from Sacramento to Colfax) as a single tan stripe with town names, if a wrap is ever done. Keep the phone number above the wheel line.
- **Required by California:** the CSLB license number on both sides of the vehicle in letters at least 72-point. The door spec above covers it.
- Keep "Formerly Northern Spark Electric" in small type under the door phone number for the first year.

## Yard signs

- 18 × 24 in corrugated, two-sided. `night` ground, lockup-dark across the top, "Electrical work by" above it in Archivo Bold `on-night-soft`, phone number in Alfa Slab One at 3 in, web address below, rust band at the bottom. Nothing else: it is read from a moving car.

## Panel stickers

- 3 × 2 in weatherproof vinyl for inside the panel door of every job: badge, "Serviced by Off 80 Electric", phone number, license, a blank line for the date and a blank line for "Work done". This is the single most effective repeat-business item an electrician has.
- See the PanelSticker template.

## Shirts and hats

- Left chest: `badge-off80.svg` at 3.5 in. Back: `lockup-dark.svg` 10 in wide on dark shirts, `lockup-light.svg` on light. Hats: badge at 2.25 in, embroidered; under 1.5 in use `mark.svg`.
- Shirt colors: `night` or a charcoal close to it, with tan thread. Avoid bright safety colors except on required hi-vis vests, where the badge goes on the back at 6 in.

## Invoices, quotes and estimates

- Letter size, `paper` ground on screen and white in print. Lockup-light top left at 2.25 in wide; business details top right in Archivo 9pt; a rust band 6pt tall under the header.
- Title "Quote" or "Invoice" in Alfa Slab One 22pt `night`. Line items in Archivo 10pt with hairline `rule` dividers; totals in Alfa Slab One. Payment terms and the license line in the footer.
- Quotes always open with the answer: the total, what it includes, and how long the job takes, before the line items.
- See the InvoiceHeader template.

## Email

- Signature: badge at 48px, name in bold, "Owner, Off 80 Electric", phone, website, license line. No quotes, no banners. See the EmailSignature template.
- Automated emails (quote confirmations and new-request alerts) use a `night` header with the badge and wordmark, a white body, steps numbered in `night` discs, and always include the "check your spam" note for info@off80electric.com.

## Social

- Profile picture: `badge-off80.png` on its own. Cover image: the home page's route scene (night sky, tan route, town names).
- Posts: 1080 × 1080. A real job photo with a `night` band at the bottom holding one short line in Alfa Slab One `tan` and the town name in Archivo `on-night`. Or, for tips, a `night` ground with a medallion illustration and the question and answer in the site's voice. See the SocialPost template.
- Captions answer first, name the town, and end with one call to action in the standard words.

## Google Business Profile and directories

- Logo: `badge-off80.png`. Cover: the route scene. Business name exactly "Off 80 Electric". Service areas: the same towns as the website. Categories: Electrician (primary), Electrical installation service, Lighting contractor, Generator shop.
- The name, phone number and city must match the website character for character on every listing (Yelp, Nextdoor, Angi, BBB, Apple, Bing).
